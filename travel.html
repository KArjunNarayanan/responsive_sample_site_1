Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple

1729 temple complex, known for its towering gateway and shrine to Padmanabha reclining on a serpent.

Periyar National Park

Reserve with a mountain, forests and lakes making a habitat for protected tigers and elephants.

Athirappilly Water Falls

Paths lead to the top & bottom of this well-known, 80-foot tall waterfall, the largest in Kerala.

Thekkady

Thekkady is a town in Kerala, India, known for its Periyar National Park, a wildlife sanctuary with diverse flora and fauna. It is famous for its spice plantations, including black pepper, cardamom, cinnamon, nutmeg, mace, ginger, and clove.

Kerala Folklore Museum

Museum of traditional local architecture as well as crafts, folk art & antiques, with a cafe & shop.

Munnar

Munnar, often called the "Kashmir of South India," is a hill station in Kerala, India, known for its tea plantations, scenic beauty, and rich biodiversity.
